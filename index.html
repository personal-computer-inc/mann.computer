Mann Computer

Agents that persist, learn their place, and work alongside people.

Mann Computer is a small research and product studio started by Christian Ervin at South Park Commons in 2026. We build scaffolding for identity-bearing agents and the simulated organizations we test them in. Alongside that, we make tools that help people keep up with what AI can do at work.

Projects

  • Scaffold

    Research

    The support structure around a base model that lets an agent hold a role over time: onboarding, memory, theory of mind, an immutable log, and simulation of actions before it takes them.

  • Consortium

    Stage 2

    A small society of autonomous agents with their own missions, sharing a treasury and a hash-chained log, coordinating through offers, commitments and handoffs.

  • Orgsim

    Testbed

    A simulated company with model-played colleagues and real feedback signals, built to measure whether an agent actually gets better at a job.

  • Pacer

    Design partners

    AI fluency training generated from each team's own work, with proficiency measured on that work instead of quiz scores.

  • Olympus Mons

    SPC Slack

    A bot that works an idea through rounds of implications until it is ten times more ambitious.

  • Hourglass

    Prototype

    An explorer for the branching pasts and futures around any token in a model's output.
